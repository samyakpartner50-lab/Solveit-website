How to center a div vertically?
I have a container with a child element, but I can't get the child to sit perfectly in the middle of the screen vertically. I've tried margin: auto but it's not working.
✅ Solution Provided

Use Flexbox! Set the container to display: flex; and add justify-content: center; and align-items: center;.

My succulent is turning yellow and mushy.
I water it once a week, but the leaves are getting soft and yellow. Is it a disease or just overwatering?
✅ Solution Provided

It is definitely overwatering. Succulents need dry soil between waterings. Stop watering immediately and check if the pot has drainage holes.
